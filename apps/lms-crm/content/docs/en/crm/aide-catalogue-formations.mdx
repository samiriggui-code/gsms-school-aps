## Role in the CRM

The **Training catalog (help)** screen (Support & quality) lists **active catalog sheets** — not a separate FAQ. For detailed procedures, use this integrated school guide (`/docs`) or the **Documentation** tab in the CRM.

## Enriching help

1. Maintain the catalog under **School life → Trainings**.
2. Handle requests under **Support → Tickets**.
3. See [Training catalog](/en/crm/catalogue-formations) and [User troubleshooting](/en/lms/depannage-utilisateurs).
