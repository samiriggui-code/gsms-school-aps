# Quotes & invoices

## Access

**Administration & billing → Finance → Quotes / Invoices**

## Link to the candidate journey

A quote can be attached to a **lead**, an **application**, or a **session**. This is not a mandatory step in the training journey.

## Usage

1. Create a quote from the lead or application record.
2. Send the client brochure.
3. On acceptance, generate the invoice.

The candidate journey (validation → session → exam → archiving) remains independent of payment.
